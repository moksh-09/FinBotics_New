Core Capabilities

Engineered for Indian Invoicing Realities

Designed specifically for dense B2B tables, multi-rate bills, missing columns, and GST compliance.

PaddleOCR Neural Engine

Uses PP-OCRv6 deep learning models optimized for complex table borders, thermal print fading, small decimals, and rupee symbols. Operates as an in-memory singleton for low CPU latency.

Dual Tax-Mode Detection

Auto-distinguishes between Tax-Inclusive Retail (where MRP includes tax) and Tax-Exclusive B2B (where tax is appended to taxable value). Back-calculates base rates automatically.

TallyPrime XML Engine

Generates standard TallyPrime purchase vouchers with automatic ledger allocation (e.g. Household Cleaning, Dairy Purchase, Input CGST @9%). Import via Gateway of Tally in 1 click.

Instant Digital PDF Fast-Path

Digital invoices downloaded from accounting software or grocery apps contain vector glyphs. FinBotics extracts coordinates directly in-browser in 18–35 ms, bypassing neural OCR entirely.

Deterministic SHA-256 Cache

Every document is content-hashed. Re-uploading or reviewing identical receipts recalls verified data in 2.9 ms from local SQLite, avoiding redundant OCR passes.

Audit & Discrepancy Desk

Flags pricing anomalies (Rate > MRP), missing vendor GSTINs, and math variances before vouchers enter your books. Edit line items inline with real-time recalculation.
